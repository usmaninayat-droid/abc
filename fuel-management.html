Fuel Receipt Template

A pre-formatted CSV with every column the system needs. One file can hold receipts for as many fleets as you like.

Columns: Date, Fleet, Vehicle / Plate No., Fuel Type, Odometer (km), Liters, Price per Liter (AED), Total Cost (AED), Station, Receipt No., Notes. Don't rename or reorder the headers.
