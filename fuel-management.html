Fuel Management

Capture actual fuel usage by importing monthly fuel receipts — the export your fuel provider (e.g. ADNOC) gives you — for whole fleets at once. Keep a per-vehicle history, turn litres into accurate consumption (litres ÷ km driven), and fall back to estimates only where no receipt or CANbus data exists.

Fuel Receipt Template

Pre-formatted CSV. Only Vehicle, Date and Litres are required — the rest is optional meta.

Columns: Vehicle / Plate No., Fleet, Date, Odometer (km), Liters, Fuel Type, Price per Liter (AED), Station, Receipt No., Notes. Date can be a day or a period (e.g. Mar 2026). Don't rename the headers.
